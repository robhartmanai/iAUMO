# iAUMO

iAUMO is a public collaboration space for ideas intended to improve collective wellbeing. Work begins as a written proposal, is reviewed in the open, and advances only when its purpose, affected communities, risks, and stewardship are clear.

## First initiative: TubmanTrain

[RFC 0001: TubmanTrain](docs/rfcs/0001-tubmantrain.md) opens a discovery process for a community-governed coordination initiative inspired by Harriet Tubman's leadership and the networks of courage around her. The name, scope, and form are proposals—not settled facts—and require review from historians, cultural stewards, organizers, safety and privacy practitioners, and people in communities the work may affect.

TubmanTrain is not accepting personal, location, migration, medical, legal, or other sensitive data. Do not use this repository to request or coordinate urgent assistance.
