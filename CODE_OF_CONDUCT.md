# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, accessible, harassment-free community. We welcome disagreement about ideas while requiring care toward people, history, and communities affected by the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and open to correction.
- Respect names, identities, boundaries, and lived experience.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- Protect private and sensitive information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and avoid implying another person's endorsement.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate misrepresentation, or retaliation are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause participation, or restrict contributors to protect the community. Do not place confidential conduct reports in a public issue. Until the project publishes a private reporting address, use GitHub's platform reporting tools for urgent abuse and open a minimal public governance issue only if it can be done without exposing sensitive details.

Enforcement decisions should be documented to the extent compatible with safety and privacy.
