# Candidate expert and steward map

This is a research list, not a list of partners or endorsements. No person or organization listed here is affiliated with iAUMO. Outreach should request advice, make no assumptions about participation, and compensate substantial labor when possible.

## Priority review roles

| Candidate | Relevant perspective | Initial request | Public contact route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harriet Tubman Underground Railroad National Historical Park (Maryland) | Tubman history, place-based interpretation, public memory | Review the historical framing and suggest appropriate community stewards | [National Park Service contact page](https://www.nps.gov/hatu/contacts.htm) |

## Additional expertise to recruit after the need is defined

- Two or more representatives chosen by the communities the project proposes to serve.
- A trauma-informed community organizer with experience in mutual aid and safeguarding.
- A privacy and security practitioner with experience protecting high-risk populations.
- An accessibility specialist and disabled participant reviewer.
- A civil-rights or public-interest attorney relevant to the eventual jurisdiction and use case.
- An open-source governance practitioner who can help establish transparent, accountable decision-making.

## Outreach guardrails

- Ask for review; never imply endorsement.
- Explain that the project is at discovery stage and may be renamed or stopped.
- Link directly to RFC 0001 and ask focused questions.
- Do not send personal or sensitive information.
- Do not publish private replies without permission.
- Offer compensation before requesting substantial work.
- Record who was invited, who responded, and what changed because of the feedback.
