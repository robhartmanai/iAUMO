# Governance

iAUMO is currently maintainer-led and RFC-driven. This is a bootstrap model, not a claim of community governance.

The repository owner is the initial maintainer. The maintainer manages access, keeps the project safe, records decisions, and may merge routine maintenance. Material choices about purpose, naming, affected communities, data, funding, deployment, or partnerships require an RFC.

Review participation does not imply endorsement. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed. When a decision materially affects a community, representatives selected by or accountable to that community must have a meaningful role before work proceeds.

As participation grows, the project should replace this bootstrap model with a governance RFC covering maintainer selection and removal, decision rights, appeals, funding transparency, community representation, and project shutdown.
